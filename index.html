How it works

Pick one thing. Do it this month.

Think of someone who could use a little encouragement or a hand. Choose something you can follow through on, then put it on your calendar or do it when the moment comes.

Do it on your own, with a friend, or with a group. Next month, pick someone or something new.

Where it started

Dusty Strickland created Randomish Kindness at North Murray High School in Chatsworth, Georgia. An unexpected postcard of encouragement inspired him to start sending his own.

School starter kit

Bring it to your school.

Start with one group. Pick one small act of kindness each month, then choose a day to do it together.

Get the printable kit
PLAN

Pick one thing for this month.

Ask a teacher, counselor, or student leader to help choose a date and an idea. A round of thank-you notes is an easy first one.

DO IT

Make someone’s day.

Use a few minutes of a class or staff meeting. If you’re writing notes, think of someone whose work might go unnoticed, like a bus driver, a cafeteria team member, or a classmate who helped someone settle in.

REPEAT

Pick next month’s date.

Ask what felt worth doing again and invite someone else to suggest the next idea. Keep participation optional and ask before sharing anyone’s note or story.
