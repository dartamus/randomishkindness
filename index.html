The story

It started with a postcard.

After Dusty spoke at a conference, someone mailed him a few words of encouragement. It showed up right when he needed it. So he began sending postcards of his own.

Later, Dusty wrote to thank the person who had sent that first note and told her about Randomish Kindness. This time, his message arrived when she needed some encouragement herself.

School starter kit

Try it next week.

Pick one group and give it a week. You’ll need a few blank cards, a place to keep them, and someone willing to write the first one.

Get the printable kit
MON

Get it off the ground.

Ask a teacher, counselor, or student leader to help. Put blank cards where your group already gathers. Write the first note yourself and deliver it privately.

MIDWEEK

Notice who you usually miss.

Invite people to thank someone whose work is easy to overlook. Try a bus driver, a library aide, a cafeteria team member, or a classmate who helped someone settle in.

FRI

Ask what felt worth repeating.

Take five minutes to ask what made sending a note easy or awkward. Choose one thing to keep next week. Let people participate privately and ask before sharing anyone’s note or story.
