Randomish Kindness

Choose one classroom, team, or department. Ask someone to help you put out blank cards and get the first notes going.

Plan your week

  • Monday. Put the cards somewhere easy to find. Deliver the first note privately.
  • Midweek. Invite the group to thank someone they might usually overlook.
  • Friday. Ask what made participating easy or awkward. Choose one thing to try again.
